## 🎯 YOUR THREE QUESTIONS - ANSWERED

### ❓ **Question 1: Will it be an issue if the training data of the model is deleted?**

### ✅ **ANSWER: NO - Absolutely Safe!**

**Explanation:**
- Models save **weights & parameters** to disk (.pth, .pkl files)
- Training data is **NOT** stored in model files
- Once trained, models are **completely independent** of training data
- You can safely delete training data anytime after training

**Proof:** See `MODEL_TRAINING_DATA_ANALYSIS.md` for detailed technical explanation
